Support report

Reproduce the problem, then prepare a report. It includes recent anonymized Frisquet responses, commands, errors and software versions.

Passwords, tokens, names, contacts, addresses and equipment identifiers are anonymized. Operating data such as temperatures and modes remains in the report. Nothing is sent until you choose to share it.

Attach the JSON file to a support issue, or send an app diagnostic report from Homey now. The prepared report is also included in the app log.
